# D-095 allowed Q1 scientific claims

## STRONGLY_SUPPORTED

- **Lower S/N reduces Green recovery in this frozen reference experiment.** On identical
  common-support GOLD transitions, mean `p_i(10)-p_i(5)` is +0.290 with 95% paired
  transition-bootstrap CI 0.172--0.416 in faint-only and +0.259 with CI 0.144--0.383 in
  matched. The conclusion does not rely on the unmatched rung populations.
- **Degrading both epochs further reduces Green recovery relative to degrading only the
  faint epoch.** On transitions supporting both arms, mean
  `p_i(faint-only)-p_i(matched)` is +0.197 (CI 0.113--0.292) at S/N 5 and +0.245
  (CI 0.141--0.362) at S/N 10.
- **Classifier choice materially affects measured recovery.** Among genuinely
  both-classifiable GOLD realization cells, Yang and Green disagree in 273/613,
  218/372, 248/695 and 184/442 cells across faint-only 5/10 and matched 5/10. Yang also
  has far larger asymmetric unclassifiability than Green.

## SUPPORTED_WITH_QUALIFICATION

- **The Green S/N direction survives the prespecified sensitivities.** GOLD+SILVER paired
  effects are +0.294/+0.268, discovery-independent Green effects are +0.298/+0.240, and
  LOTO/LOSFO effects remain positive in faint-only/matched. Magnitude is not invariant.
- **The Green S/N effect is heterogeneous across transitions.** Faint-only has 18
  positive, eight zero and one negative paired changes; matched has 18 positive, six
  zero and one negative. Ranges are −0.02 to +0.88 and −0.06 to +0.96.
- **Source-family composition affects the magnitude of Green recovery and its S/N
  contrast.** Equal-family paired effects are +0.180 faint-only and +0.303 matched,
  versus primary +0.290/+0.259. Removing Dong2025 attenuates faint-only to +0.215; no
  matched-arm family removal attenuates by more than 0.020.
- **Yang supplies a validity-limited classifier-specific result.** Its frozen recovery
  values may be reported with their exact classifiable denominators and U, but they do
  not establish S/N invariance or a population curve.

## NOT_SUPPORTED

- **Yang shows the same S/N dependence as Green.** Its paired effects are centered near
  zero and most eligible rows are unclassifiable; boundary failures dominate.
- **The measured literature-CLAGN recovery fractions are population rates.** The sample
  is a locked reference set, source-family composition is uneven, and equal-family
  weighting materially moves estimates.
- **Host dilution has a universal scalar effect.** D-084 froze the controlled-host result
  as shape-dependent; D-095 supplies no basis to replace that conclusion.
- **S/N selection alone explains published CLAGN demographics.** Q1 isolates one
  counterfactual measurement mechanism and does not identify the full demographic
  selection function.
- **A universal classifier-independent completeness curve is supported.** Yang/Green
  disagreement and asymmetric unclassifiability directly contradict this claim.
- **MacLeod provides a scalar Q1 recovery curve here.** Its required visual-final evidence
  was unavailable prospectively, so it remains entirely non-denominator.
